The Maison

A house built on craft

Varenne began as a small tailoring atelier on the Rue de Varenne in Paris, and took its name from the street. Today we work with family-run mills and workshops across Italy and Scotland, producing each collection in limited numbers.

We believe a garment should be worn for decades, so we design with restraint and choose materials that age well.
